Course information

Six courses, fully represented

Each course is modelled with its instructor, schedule, room, prerequisites, topic list, and assessment weights. Open any card for the complete record — the same data the assistant reasons over.

Classroom rules

Fifteen rules, and the reason behind each

Policy without rationale is just friction. Every rule carries its severity, its exact wording, and a short justification. Filter by category or search the full text.

Learning & support workflows

The path from a problem to a resolution

Eight documented workflows covering both sides of student life: how to learn well, and how to get help when something goes wrong. Each has an owner, a service-level expectation, and numbered steps.

Modern AI paradigms

Nine approaches, scored against this problem

Each paradigm is judged on what a classroom assistant actually needs: grounded answers, explainability, low latency, and policy safety. The fit score is a weighted judgement, not a benchmark — and the verdict says where each one belongs.

The assistant

Ask anything about this classroom

Two engines, chosen by env.js. Local AI runs entirely in your browser with TF-IDF retrieval and intent rules — no key, no network. Gemini, once its key is set there, thinks through any question in any language with the knowledge base as grounding, and falls back to Local AI if a call fails.

Role-based portal

One assistant. Access that is issued, not guessed.

Sign in and Aria opens the workspace that matches the role on your account. Nothing is self-selected and no panel is advertised on the sign-in page — management and administration issue access, staff and students can join with Google, and every account carries its own history.
